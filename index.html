01 / About

Built it before anyone believed in it.

Brand BuilderMENA · Esports

I built Pegasus Esports from a blank page into one of Egypt's most recognized esports brands — no investors, no paid media, no team behind me at the start. Just strategy, content and relentless execution.

Over three years, Pegasus reached 1.2M+ monthly impressions organically, shipped merchandise to 7 countries, and developed creators who became some of the most influential voices in MENA gaming.

It's not only zero-to-one. At Ink Plus and Top Ink I stepped into running businesses and, in six months, more than doubled views to 7.3M (+106%) — and made Ink Plus the share-of-voice leader in its market. At Glacial Globals in the US, I ran the creative system behind a 5M+ view campaign.

"Give me nothing and I'll build it. Give me something and I'll scale it."
